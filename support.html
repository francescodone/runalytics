Common questions

Why is a recent workout missing?

Check that your watch has synced to its provider, then open Watch connections and sync again. Keep the app open until the sync completes.

How do I move a session?

Open the session in Calendar and choose Move to. Scheduling safeguards may refuse dates that put hard sessions too close together.

Has my plan reached my watch?

Check the plan sync status on Plan. A saved local change is not necessarily installed on your watch. Failed publishing can be retried without recreating your plan.

What do the recovery signals mean?

They describe trends in your synced data, not a medical diagnosis. Log pain in Injuries and seek medical advice for persistent or concerning symptoms.
